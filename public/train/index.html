Train

Run and lift, side by side, for the long haul.

Train is an iOS app for planning, scheduling and tracking hybrid training: aerobic work and strength. It's built on one belief: consistent training in both keeps athletes of every level healthier, less injury-prone and performing better much later in life.

What it does

  • Plan your cardio. Schedule runs and other aerobic sessions on a weekly calendar, including recurring plans.
  • Plan your strength. Lay out lifts on a weekly board and log each session quickly.
  • Track progress. Review your history, mileage and how each session felt.
  • On-device AI coach. Ask about your training by typing or speaking. The model runs entirely on your iPhone.
  • Your data, your way. Import workouts from Apple Health or CSV, export to CSV, and optionally keep a copy in a Google Sheet.

Privacy

Your training data lives on your device and in your own private iCloud. Train has no accounts, ads or analytics, and its developer runs no servers that receive your data. Read the full privacy policy.
