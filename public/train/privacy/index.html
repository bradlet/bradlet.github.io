Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

In short: Train keeps your training data on your device. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and its developer runs no servers that receive your data. Data leaves your device only when you use a feature that needs it, as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your planned and recorded workouts, strength plans and lift records, notes, how-you-felt and perceived-exertion ratings, and app preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. Train does not currently sync this data to iCloud or any other cloud service. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Apple Health

If you choose to import from Apple Health, Train asks for read-only access to your workouts and copies the workouts you select into the app on your device. Train never writes to Apple Health. Health data is not shared with anyone and is never used for advertising or marketing.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you dictate a message to the AI coach, Train uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to turn your speech into text. Where your device supports it, recognition happens entirely on-device. Otherwise, Apple processes the audio under Apple's privacy policy. Train does not record or store your audio.

AI coach

The AI coach runs entirely on your iPhone. Your conversations and training data are never sent off the device. The language model is downloaded once from Hugging Face, and only after you tap to start the download. That is an ordinary file download: Hugging Face receives the standard request details (such as your IP address), but none of your training data.

Google Sheets sync (optional)

Sheets sync is off by default. If you turn it on, you sign in with Google and Train requests:

  • Your basic Google profile (name, email address and profile picture). Google Sign-In provides this; Train does not store or use it beyond completing sign-in.
  • Access to the files Train creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file scope). Train creates one spreadsheet and writes your recorded workout history to it (date, name, type, duration, distance, notes and ratings), overwriting it after each change. This scope does not let Train see or change any other file in your Drive.

Your workout data goes directly from your device to Google. It never passes through any server run by Train's developer. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain on your device. You can turn sync off in the app at any time, remove Train's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the spreadsheet from your Drive yourself.

Train's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the Sheets sync you turned on. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

CSV import and export

Importing reads only the file you pick. Exporting creates a file that goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Sharing, selling and tracking

Train does not sell your data, share it with third parties except as described above, show ads, or track you across apps and websites.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes it. Copies you made yourself, such as a Google Sheet or exported files, stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children

Train is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
